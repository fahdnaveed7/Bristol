UNDERSTANDING THE PRODUCTS

Three forms of marine value.

PROTEIN

Fish meal

Steam drying produces a concentrated protein ingredient. Grade selection considers protein, fat, moisture and ash.

LIPIDS

Fish oil

Separation and subsequent processing create oils for different applications. The required profile depends on the end use.

SOLUBLES

Soluble paste

Concentrating soluble fractions produces a paste containing dissolved proteins and amino acids.
